Divya Auto Hub Pvt. Ltd. is the authorised Bajaj Motorcycle dealership in Maharajgunj, Kathmandu — stocking the complete Bajaj range, from the daily-workhorse Platina to the dominant NS400.

Our showroom is a single point of contact for the purchase, finance, exchange, servicing, insurance and spare parts of every Bajaj motorcycle sold in Nepal. The team behind the counter has been with the brand for over a decade — they know the differences between an NS200 and an N250, and they will tell you which one fits your commute.

Whether you are buying your first motorcycle or your fifth, the brief is the same: a hassle-free experience, transparent pricing, and after-sales support that does not disappear the moment the registration plate is fitted.
